Konnichiwa mina-san~

I managed somehow to finish my Hina zine for Resin Rose. I designed it for a laserjet printer, which has trouble with multiple colors but not as much trouble with black. I knew it was not going to be able to handle photos, so everything is greyscale with pink accents.

I made 4 pages in Krita and then linked them to an Inkscape template that was included with my install of Inkscape. It's really cool how the pages get linked and then cloned! I have no idea how to set that up.

While printing prototypes, I noticed that there was no way to get the pages perfectly aligned. One of the pages was just a sliver over which was obvious when printing mostly black pages! I ended up making my own grid in Inkscape and it printed better.

The worst thing happened when I started printing the final copies... TONER LOW??? Only 5 copies in?! We ordered a new printer because apparently the toner carts for this printer are $300 somehow.

I remembered seeing some redditor (ikr....) say that there are no sample toner carts and only full size and that you should reset the print counter when you get the printer. So we tried it and I was able to print 20 copies for Saturday! The print quality definitely started decreasing so I fear the low toner warning was indeed correct.

I handed out 16 on Saturday and printed and made 10 more that night on the new printer. I actually got really good at my makeshift assembly line, if you will. I think I can handle mass producing these now. 1000 Hina zines?!
